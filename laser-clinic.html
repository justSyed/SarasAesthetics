Common Questions

How many sessions will I need?
Most clients require between 6 to 8 sessions to achieve 90% hair reduction. This varies depending on your hair thickness and skin type.
Does it hurt?
Our modern laser machines use cooling technology to minimize discomfort. Most clients describe the sensation as a light flick of a rubber band.
Is a patch test required?
Yes, a mandatory patch test is required 24 hours before your first treatment to ensure your skin is suitable for the laser.
What should I do before my appointment?
Please shave the area to be treated 24 hours before your appointment. Avoid sun exposure and fake tan for at least 2 weeks prior.
